# Content completion checklist

Nothing below was invented. Items marked **verify** exist only as drafts and are hidden in production until you confirm them and switch **Facts verified** on.

## Profile (Studio → Personal profile)
- [ ] Upload a real portrait photograph (with alt text). No AI-generated portraits.
- [ ] Add a public contact email
- [ ] Create Social links (GitHub, LinkedIn) and attach them
- [ ] Upload resume PDF
- [ ] Review the biography, philosophy and working principles copy; edit to your own voice
- [ ] Optional availability message

## Experience (Studio → Career → Experience)
- [ ] PrimeQA Solutions: start date, confirm employment type, responsibilities, verified achievements, technologies, related projects
- [ ] Freelance Software Engineer: dates (overlap allowed), engagement types, verified projects
- [ ] Sapphire Software Solution: dates, location, responsibilities, technologies, achievements

## Education & certifications
- [ ] B.Tech CSE, Indus University: add start/end years
- [ ] Add certifications (if any) with credential URLs
